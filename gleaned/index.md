## Gleanings

- [A Command to Flee](./A-Command-to-Flee): The apostle Paul uses the verb (flee: φεύγω) four times in his writings. 
- [Beware](./Beware): The Lord Jesus constantly warned his followers to watch out, beware, be careful, or be on guard. The greek verb used for this, προσέχω, can be seen across the gospels in several places.
- [Peters-Folly-Pauls-Difficulty](./Peters-Folly-Pauls-Difficulty): When Peter and the other apostles presented Matthias as a replacement for Judas' apostleship, they did not realize that they were making an error. 
- [Peters-Keys-and-the-Baptism-of-the-Spirit](./Peters-Keys-and-the-Baptism-of-the-Spirit): The seemingly unimportant yet widespread misunderstanding today about the difference between "baptism of the Spirit" and the "filling of the Spirit", often leads to serious confusions. 
- [The-Friends-and-Foes-of-Jesus](./The-Friends-and-Foes-of-Jesus): The scriptures record that during His time here on earth, the Lord directly addressed only a few as friends.
- [Theleema](./Theleema): Often when faced with difficult choices, one obsesses about what really is the will of God.
