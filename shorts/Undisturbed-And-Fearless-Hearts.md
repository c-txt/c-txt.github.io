# Undisturbed And Fearless Hearts

> Peace I leave with you. My peace I give to you; not as the world gives, give I to you. Don’t let your heart be troubled, neither let it be fearful.  - John 14:27

The Lord said the peace He was giving was not like what the world was giving. What is the peace that the world gives? It is temporary. It is treacherous. It quickly fades. We often go to great lengths to gain peace. We undertake religous programs, pilgrimages and performances, we go on expensive holidays, we submit ourselves to massages and spa treatments, we numb the fears of the soul through parties, pleasures, doomscrolling, and entertainment, we pursue money and power hoping to use them to solve our problems. We go to great lengths to gain friendships and acceptance in society hoping to find peace in the company of others. Yet, through all this, we quickly realize that all of the world's portals and promises of peace fail to deliver much other than eventual emptiness and disillussionment. And instead of peace we end up with troubles. 

So, what did the peace of the Lord look like? It was a peace that surpassed understanding. It was a peace that was constant through the great and constant trials of His life. It was a peace that was not dependent on His external circumstances. For Jesus did not have an easy life. He was dogged at every step by people who hated Him. He was constantly being questioned and put down. He was always being misunderstood by those closest to Him. His family thought He was out of His mind. Many called Him demon possesed. He was dirt poor, with no money even to pay His taxes. People were picking up stones to throw at Him. But His peace was in God the Father. His every need was met. He was protected at every turn. His peace rested in the knowledge that nothing could come to Him or be prevented from coming to Him except through the will of His heavenly Father.

Today, when we look around, there are many reasons to be troubled. There have been waves of pandemics, there are rumours of global war, there are economic uncertainties looming, and much else. The world constantly tempts us with its pathways to escape these troubles and gain peace.  But even as the Lord Jesus told His disciples that He was giving them His peace (in the present continuous tense), we too have His promise today. Only we must not misunderstand what He is promising.

Jesus never promised freedom from trials. But He promised the peace He Himself had. While the world promises us peace through the solution of our problems or an escape from our problems, He promises us peace through our problems. And, while He keeps supplying us that peace through the fruit of the Holy Spirit, experiencing it requires us to do our part. He said “Let not your heart be troubled, neither let it be afraid.” And so, let us not quench the Spirit with our worries and fears. May we enjoy His peace through all the changing circumstances of our lives. Amen!
